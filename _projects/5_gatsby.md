As part of the MSc in Machine Learning programme at UCL, I had the opportunity to take two modules at the Gatsby Unit aimed at their Ph.D. students. Each module had a substantial piece of coursework, linked below.

**Module 1: Probabilistic and Unsupervised Learning**

Topics: text decryption with MCMC, the EM algorithm, Gibbs sampling for LDA, model selection, optimisation.

[Question Paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dgVLjtE_3-_wsRo0h3i4_2yMZuMWkCmD/view?usp=sharing) &middot; [My Coursework](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bIlHcsZ58J_u_cgImLloUsHEABlFfeJs/view?usp=sharing)

**Module 2: Approximate Inference and Learning in Probabilistic Models**

Topics: graphical models, Gaussian processes, variational inference, expectation propagation, belief propagation.
